Decide first, send together

How review works

From submission to notice

  1. Mentor approval. An abstract enters review once the mentor approves it. Faculty submissions enter directly.
  2. Review. Any reviewer opens an abstract, reads the automatic checks and saves a decision with comments.
  3. Second look. Anyone on the committee can open a saved decision and change it until notices are sent. The page shows who decided each one.
  4. Notices. Any committee member sends all saved decisions at once from dcgsedr@augusta.edu, with the mentor copied.
  5. Competition. Accepted abstracts entered in the competition are placed in judging groups and numbered for the poster session.

Decisions
